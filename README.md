### `> what I do`

- 🛡️ **CyberSec education at scale** — talks, classrooms and content that make security and compliance *operational*, not theoretical, for the people defending small and mid-sized business.
- 🎙️ **[Beard Banter](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/cybermattlee)** — candid conversations with the people shaping the industry.
- 🧰 **"How I Would Hack You"** — attacker methods, and how to defend against them, in plain English.
- 🤝 Building community wherever MSPs, SMBs and security people gather.

### `> raising the tide`

- 🎖️ **Veterans into cyber** — funding laptops and certifications for exiting members of the armed forces, helping them skill up and land in the field.
- 🌊 A rising tide lifts every boat. Knowledge shared *is* the tide.

### `> off the clock`

- 🏍️ **Two wheels, preferably dirty** — dual-sport and dirt bikes; the best threat models get worked out somewhere off the pavement.
- 🧔 The beard has its own brand. And, occasionally, its own charity.
